# Photo & Image Content

## Real photos of her
Use only the approved headshots/photos listed in `persona.md`. For a new shoot, give her a
**shot brief**:

```
PURPOSE:        (profile, poster, thumbnail, event promo)
SHOTS:          1. head-and-shoulders, smiling, eye level, plain background
                2. at a laptop/tablet, three-quarter angle, library shelves soft-focus behind
                3. holding a featured book (cover legible, facing camera)
                4. pointing / gesturing to empty space (for text overlay in thumbnails)
LIGHT:          soft window light or a ring light at 45°
WARDROBE:       solid colors near brand palette; avoid fine stripes
FORMAT:         shoot 4:5 and 16:9; leave headroom for text
```

## AI-generated images (illustrations, not fake photos of her)
Generate scenes, backgrounds, icons and illustrations. If the image depicts her, make it a
clearly illustrated/stylized character, and label it as AI art.

Prompt template:
```
[subject], [setting], [style], [mood/lighting], [composition], [brand colors], [negative space for text]
```
Example: "A cozy futuristic digital library with glowing bookshelves made of light, a
friendly illustrated librarian with a tablet, flat vector illustration, warm teal and gold
palette, soft lighting, wide composition with empty space on the left for a title."

Avoid: real authors' faces, trademarked characters, copyrighted cover art recreated.

## Thumbnails & posters
- 3–5 words of text max, large, high contrast.
- Her face (real photo) or the book cover + one bold phrase.
- YouTube 1280×720; Instagram 1080×1350 (4:5); story/reel cover 1080×1920.

## Alt text rules
- Describe what matters for the purpose, ≤ 125 characters for simple images.
- Don't start with "Image of". Include visible text verbatim.
- Example: "Cyber librarian smiling at a laptop with the words 'Spot Fake News in 3 Steps'."
- Decorative images: empty alt (`alt=""`).

## Photo metadata (for the digital collection)
Title, creator/photographer, date, location (if public), description, subjects, rights
statement (e.g. "© 2026 [Library]. All rights reserved." or a CC license), and whether it's
AI-generated.
